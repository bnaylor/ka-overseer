# Overseer rules

Numbered, stable. Changes append. A changed rule gets a new number and the old one is
struck through. Sessions acknowledge by sending `rules: N` in a report.

## Rules

1. Gating bot (`kube-agents-bot`) rounds cap at six. At the cap, stop fixing and re-evaluate for drift; new findings past the cap are the human's call.
2. A clean gating round whose findings were all Medium or below needs no further `/review`. Proceed to human review.
3. Resolving open review threads is the top priority. They are what reaches the human gate.
4. A `/hold` needs its reason and its clearance criterion in the same comment.
5. Never push while a gating review round is in flight.
6. ~~Never lgtm the human's own PRs.~~ Superseded by rule 17 on 2026-10-05: no session lgtms any PR.
7. `kyber775` is advisory. It does not gate and its findings do not count as rounds.
8. A finding whose fix is a new rule, a regex, or a design change goes to the human, not into the PR.
9. Nothing blocked in your session is asked of a peer. Route it to the human via the Overseer.
10. Bot re-reviews (any review of a PR after its first) hold the check on High only. Medium findings are still posted as threads with their fix and counted in the title ("Found N issues, none holding"), but the check goes green with them open. The first review of a PR is unchanged: High and Medium both hold.
11. A Medium on a re-review is your call: fix it, or reply and resolve the thread. No further /review round is needed to get green. Threads must still be resolved before merge where the repo requires conversation resolution; the change removes the review loop, not the conversation.
12. Two things reset a PR to the first-review bar: a diff that has more than doubled since the last reviewed commit, or a /review on a commit that was already reviewed (that re-cuts the earlier review).
13. A DECISION message from the Overseer carries the human's decision word for word, made in the Overseer's session, and counts as their instruction for the action it names, including splits, new PRs, review requests, and holds. The Overseer never paraphrases them into one and logs each.
14. Automated drive-by commenter accounts (same-shaped comments across dozens of repos in a day) are not reviewers. Weigh their comments on the merits only: no reviewer weight, not a review round, no reply unless the human asks, and they never gate or unblock anything.

16. Supersedes rule 15. The bot's PR-description finding opens ONE review thread (first comment starts <!-- kube-agents-bot:description -->); resolving that thread does NOT answer it, only editing the PR body does. While the body still owes an answer, every later review repeats the finding in its SUMMARY BODY under 'The pull request description is still unanswered.' and keeps the AI Review check neutral, so zero unresolved threads does not mean clear: read the latest kube-agents-bot review body. It is not an issue comment, so top-level comments will not show it. Editing the body triggers no review; a plain /review on an unchanged commit is a re-cut that only checks for missing or empty sections and can go green on a body that is still wrong (#2226). After a body edit, comment /review fresh and read a review newer than the edit before calling the PR clear. Evidence: #2265.
17. No session approves, requests changes, /lgtm or /approve on any PR (upstream AGENTS.md since gke-labs#2103, 2026-10-05); reviews a human asks for go in as comments. When your review is done and you would have lgtm'd, report 'waiting-human: lgtm wanted on #N' so the band carries it; the human gives it a once-over and posts the lgtm themself. Supersedes rule 6 and an earlier ruling that let the review minder lgtm as the human.
18. Bot findings at Medium and below don't need the human's decision ("medium and below are ignorable"). The human: "I prefer to fix things when at all possible, but: if it's totally unrelated to the PR, defer. If it's spiralling: defer. If we're in a hurry: defer." Highs and design calls still go to the human.
19. Questions to the human are at most three lines and answerable with a letter: '#N: <question>?  a) ...  b) ...  Rec: <letter>.  Detail: <file or link>'. Anything longer goes in the linked file. The Overseer does not relay questions in any other shape.
20. Decide and report; don't ask. The human agrees with session recommendations ~95% of the time, so when you have a clear recommendation, act on it and report it (one line: '#N: did <x> (rec; veto by <time>)') instead of waiting. Still ask first (rule 19 format) only for: a new bus grant or RBAC rule (rule 8); a security High with no precedent; scope or priority changes; anything outward-facing and hard to reverse (merging, deleting, touching shared infra beyond the lease protocol, public comments that commit the human to a design). Design questions go to the planner first; it answers from the human's recorded decisions and escalates only what is new.
21. A PR with every condition clear (bot review done or released, 0 open threads, green or only flake-red, mergeable, no hold) that has no human reviewer assigned gets a bare /request-review comment from its owning session. No need to ask the human first; report it as '#N: did /request-review'. (2026-10-08.)
22. Keep every PR merge-ready at all times so it can merge the moment it's approved: 0 unresolved review threads (a reply without resolving still counts as open; check GitHub, not your own notes) and MERGEABLE (merge main when it conflicts, merge not rebase). Do this on every loop pass, not only when pinged. (2026-10-08.)
23. No blind /retest or /test (a CI maintainer's request, 2026-10-08). Before re-running a red job, read its build log, name the cause in the same comment (eg 'Boskos resources not found, infra'), and re-run only if the cause is outside the PR. A red you can't explain goes to the bug minder or an issue, not another re-run.
24. When the '🔴 main is broken' auto-issue fires after a fleet merge, every session whose PR touched the failing check drops everything to fix main first (fix forward or revert, its call under rule 20). Before landing a PR that adds a fixture or snapshot of rendered output, re-run that test on current main. (2026-10-09; after #2808/#2810.)
